What TrackLink does

Everything a team needs to stay in sync

Two simple roles — Track and Hub — cover the whole job: drivers report their position, and the business sees exactly where everyone is.

Live GPS tracking

Track users toggle tracking on and forget it — a persistent notification confirms it's running while location uploads in the background at a chosen interval.

Live team map

Hub users see every device on one map, refreshing automatically, with under 5-second latency from upload to marker move — no manual refresh needed.

Route history

Review the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days for any device — a full route with stats, kept for 30 days by default or 365 days with the retention add-on.

Custom groups

Organise Track users into your own groups — "Motorcycle fleet", "Car fleet", whatever fits — then filter the map and device list by group in one tap.

Multi-Hub organisations

Add as many Hub (admin) users as you need — every Hub sees every device, so dispatchers and managers all work from the same live picture.

Isolated by design

Every organisation's data is walled off in the database's own security rules — not just filtered on screen — so one business can never see another's.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is TrackLink available yet?

TrackLink's tracking, live map and billing are built and running in production. We're finishing public sign-up rollout, so get in touch and we'll get your team set up directly.

What does TrackLink cost?

TrackLink starts at £18/month for 1 Hub seat plus 3 Track seats, with a 14-day free trial. Extra Track and Hub seats come in packs, and an optional add-on extends history retention from 30 to 365 days — see current pricing above.

How often does TrackLink update a device's location?

The standard cadences are 30 seconds, 60 seconds or 5 minutes. A 5-second high-frequency mode and a 1-second Live Track mode are also available as explicit opt-ins for near-live tracking, with in-app battery and data guidance so you know the trade-off before you turn them on.

Is our team's location data isolated from other companies?

Yes. Every organisation's data is isolated by a unique organisation ID and enforced in the database's own security rules, not just by the app — one business can never read or write another business's data.

What happens if a device loses signal?

Fixes queue on the device and flush automatically once it reconnects — even after an extended period offline, nothing is lost, it just arrives a little later.

Can more than one person manage the team?

Yes — an organisation can have any number of Hub users, and every Hub sees every Track device. You can also organise Track users into custom groups (e.g. "Motorcycle fleet") and filter the map by group.
